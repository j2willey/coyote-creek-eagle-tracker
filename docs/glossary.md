# Glossary

Terms used by the Coyote Creek District Advancement Committee and throughout this repository, from a Life Scout's first contact through the Eagle Board of Review. *Avoid* lists words we deliberately don't use, because they are ambiguous or misleading.

## Language

### People and bodies

**Scout**:
A Life Scout working toward Eagle who has made contact with the district.
_Avoid_: candidate (until a term is settled), applicant

**Advancement Chair**:
The district volunteer who receives intake email and keeps the tracking sheet and drive.
_Avoid_: CC chair

**Committee Member**:
A district advancement volunteer who reviews proposals and sits on EBoRs. Any member may do Intake Checks and Pre-Review Calls.

**Review Panel**:
The Committee Members (ideally ≥2) at an in-person Proposal Review, deliberately not the member who ran the Pre-Review Call where possible, so the Scout presents to a fresh audience.
_Avoid_: Lead Reviewer (rejected: one owner per Scout undercuts the fresh-audience presentation)

**Council**:
Silicon Valley Monterey Bay Council (SVMBC) staff, who verify Eagle Applications and record the rank.

**Beneficiary**:
The organization the Eagle Project serves, which must sign the Proposal.

**Committee Group**:
The district advancement Google Group that every Scout email should copy. Its archive is the shared record of each Scout's correspondence.
_Avoid_: the list, the alias

**Unit**:
A Scout's troop, crew or ship, written type letter + number: **T910** (Troop), **C910** (Venturing Crew), **S910** (Sea Scout Ship). Troops (~95%) and Crews (~5%) often share a number, so the letter is required. Colloquially "Unit Number" means the whole thing.
_Avoid_: a bare number ("910")

**Unit Leader**:
The Scoutmaster or equivalent adult in the Scout's unit, who owns nudging a Scout during the Quiet Period.

### Artifacts and events

**Candidate Record**:
The committee's record of one Scout's path from the first submission through EBoR: a row in the tracking sheet (Record ID in the hidden `_key` column), created when an intake submission is promoted (or by hand).
_Avoid_: row, entry, file

**Scout Update**:
A change a Scout submits to their own Candidate Record: a revised Proposal, or corrected contact or project information. It never changes an outcome (approval, denial, EBoR result).
_Avoid_: resubmission (a Scout Update can carry one, but it is the broader term)

**Proposal**:
The scout's Eagle Service Project Proposal, signed by beneficiary, unit leader, and unit committee before it reaches the district.
_Avoid_: project (that is the work itself)

**Proposal Guide**:
The district's preparation guide and submission checklist handed to Life Scouts before they submit a Proposal (file naming, required pages, maps and photos, size limits).
_Avoid_: rubric (it guides the Scout; it is not a scoring sheet)

**Intake Check**:
The committee's completeness check of a Proposal in **Proposal Received** (all pages, signatures, contact info). Something missing → **Proposal Incomplete**; ready → **Intake Review: To Book**.
_Avoid_: preliminary review

**Pre-Review Call**:
A short (15–20 min) Google Meet call between the committee and a Scout, before the Proposal Review, to go over notable issues and prepare the Scout to present.
_Avoid_: pre-review, coaching call, prescreen (it suggests a gate the Scout can fail; the call is coaching)

**Proposal Review**:
A district meeting where at least two Committee Members review a Proposal and approve it or send it back.

**Revise and Resubmit**:
A Proposal Review outcome in which the Scout must change the Proposal before approval. Usually closed by email; sometimes needs a follow-up meeting.
_Avoid_: rejected, failed

**Denial**:
A Proposal Review outcome in which the project cannot meet requirements and the Scout needs a different project.
_Avoid_: unapproved (the sheet's "Unapproved Proposals" section mixes denial and pending states)

**Closed**:
The terminal stage (side section, last on the tracking tab) for a Candidate Record that will not come back: withdrew, aged out (no extension), extension denied, moved out of district, duplicate or error, or other. A member closes it from an Exceptions stage (**Close record…**) and can **Reopen…** it back to where it came from. Never emailed automatically; the Scout's page says "Your record is closed." Shown on the dashboard only behind "Show closed (n)". The committee decides: the tool only suggests (**Close?** tag) and never closes by itself.
_Avoid_: archived (that is the January step), deleted, rejected

**Closed Archive**:
The tab of the tracking spreadsheet that the January archive appends to: one row per Closed record from a past year (Record ID, Scout, unit, closed date, reason, note, who closed it, stage before closing, Proposal approved date, EBoR date, folder link, archived on). Deliberately **no contact details** (no emails, phones, birth date, address, BSA ID): these are minors' records, and the Scout's folder (moved to `Archive/Closed`) holds the paperwork.

**Eagle Archive**:
The tab of the tracking spreadsheet that holds past years' Eagles: one "<year> Eagles" section per year, newest at the top, each followed by that year's Scouts (all columns, with the folder links). The January archive adds the new year at the top, matching columns by machine name. Not to be confused with the Closed Archive (Closed records only).

**Quiet Period**:
The span between Proposal approval and Eagle Application submission, when the district usually hears nothing.

**Age-Out Check**:
A periodic look for Scouts nearing their 18th birthday (≈17.5+) with no Eagle Application, triggering one check-in via the Unit Leader.

**Review Slot**:
A dated time at a place where the committee can hold a session. Its use (Proposal Review or EBoR) is decided by what gets booked, not fixed in advance.
_Avoid_: EBoR slot (for an unbooked slot)

**Slot Type**:
The code in a Review Slot's type cell: **Intake** (Pre-Review Call), **ESSPPR** (Proposal Review), **EBoR**, **Followup** (a later review with a Scout after the first), **Blocked** (also written **CCAC**: a committee meeting, holiday or other time no session may be offered; no time = the whole day). Each slot also has a hidden key `SL_yymmdd_XXX` that never changes, even if the slot is moved.

**Initials**:
How Committee Members are written in Who / Rep columns (full names make columns too wide). Several allowed, separated by `,` or `/` (e.g. `JW/AB`): one member creates a slot, others add their initials to join. The Committee tab maps initials to name and email.

**District Rep**:
A Committee Member who self-signs onto a Review Slot to sit on that session.
_Avoid_: council rep (the sheet says "Council or District Rep", but these are district volunteers)

**Unit-Hosted EBoR**:
An EBoR held at a unit's own meeting night and location, arranged ad hoc with the Unit when a District Rep is available.

**Booking**:
A Scout's claim on a Review Slot for one specific session type (Pre-Review Call, Proposal Review, or EBoR), allowed only once the Scout has reached that stage.
_Avoid_: signup (ambiguous between Scouts booking and reps self-signing)

**Unit Info tab**:
One row per unit (T910 / C917 / S905): whether it is under review (Board Restriction), whether it is a Lien Doan unit, where and when it meets, and notes. Maintained by CCAC members.

**Lien Doan**:
A Unit Info column (check box) marking units that belong to a Lien Doan: a collective group of units from, supporting and supported by the Vietnamese community. A group, not a person.

**Key 3 tab**:
Council's Key 3 export (unit leader, committee chair, chartered org rep per unit), replaced on each import and never edited by hand. Its Unit Leader and Committee Chair are unit contacts.

**Unit Contacts tab**:
Unit contacts from anywhere but the Key 3 (EBoR Coordinator, Advancement Chair, Other), one row per contact. Role *Exclude* hides an address. One adult may be a contact for several units.

**Unit Board Members**:
The 2–4 troop committee members a Unit adds to an EBoR after being notified of the Scout's Booking.

**Board Restriction**:
A temporary rule on a Unit that its Scouts' EBoRs may include only District and Council reps, with no Unit Board Members.

**Eagle Application**:
The rank application plus supporting documents, submitted to Council after the project is done.

**Formsite**:
The Council's online form service where Scouts submit the Eagle Application and its documents. It emails a result (with attachments) to the committee's Council account, and the tool files it into the Scout's folder (see **Council application**).

**Verification**:
Council's confirmation that an Eagle Application is complete and the scout is eligible for an EBoR.

**Unit EBoR Coordinator**:
The unit adult who arranges a unit's side of an EBoR. By default, the **Unit Advancement Coordinator** listed on the Proposal's contact page. In small units it is often the Unit Leader, and occasionally a different adult (override per unit).
_Avoid_: unit contact

**Unit Contacts**:
The unit adults the committee notifies about a Booking: the Unit EBoR Coordinator (if one is known), the Unit Leader (Scoutmaster/Crew Advisor) and the Unit Committee Chair. Who in the unit picks the Unit Board Members is the unit's business, not the committee's.

**EBoR Reminders**:
The one email (4.3.c) a committee member sends as an EBoR approaches, To the Scout, the district reps and the Unit Board Members, Cc the parent/guardian and the committee: date, time, place, who is on the board, what the Scout brings and wears. Separate from the earlier Booking notice to Unit Contacts. (Two emails until 2026-11-02.)

**EBoR**:
Eagle Board of Review, the final rank review, with at least one district member present.
_Avoid_: board, BOR

**Eagle Workbook**:
The Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook, containing the Proposal, Plan and Report sections.
_Avoid_: project packet

**Life Purpose Statement**:
The Scout's "Statement of Life Goals and Ambitions" submitted with the Eagle Application.

**LOR**:
A letter of recommendation submitted by a reference through the Council site.
Confidential: shown to boards only as physical printouts, never to the Scout.
_Avoid_: reference letter

**Proposal Workflow**:
From the Scout's first submission through Intake Check, Pre-Review Call, Proposal Review and approval of the Proposal. Stands alone. **Phase 1** of the tracker.

**EBoR Workflow**:
From the Eagle Application through Verification, Booking and the EBoR. Almost stands alone, but needs an existing Candidate Record. **Phase 2** of the tracker, together with Unit Contacts and EBoR scheduling.

**Intake Queue**:
Where every intake submission lands first, confirmed or not, before it becomes a Candidate Record. Committee members follow up on unconfirmed submissions or delete spam there.
_Avoid_: holding pen

**AI Pre-Check**:
An automated first look at a submitted Proposal for missing signatures and contact info, before the Intake Check. Flags gaps; a human stays in the loop.

### Tool terms (added 2026-10-21)

**Section key**:
The fixed name of a tracking-sheet section (`intake_to_book`, `review_booked`, ...) stored as `S:<key>` in the hidden `_key` column. The visible label can be renamed in Settings; the key never changes.

**Committee schedule**:
The committee's calendar page (`/committee/schedule`): month/week view of every Review Slot on the schedule sheet, where members create, change and delete sessions and manage booked ones. The schedule sheet stays the record.
_Avoid_: the calendar (Google Calendar holds only booked sessions)

**Create and book**:
A member creates a slot and puts a Scout in it in one step (after agreeing the time with the family). No online cutoff applies; a Scout already booked for that type is moved.

**Blocked time**:
A schedule entry (meeting, holiday) during which no session is offered to Scouts. Never creates an invite.

**Occasional helper**:
A person on the Committee tab with **Active** unticked: can be put on a session (and is invited), can sign in, but is listed separately so the main list stays short.

**Email template**:
One tab of the template Google Doc: `Subject`, `Mode` (Automatic / Review / Off), `Cc`, then the wording with `{placeholders}`.

**Outbox**:
Where an email caused by a committee member waits (when its template says Mode: Review) until a member sends it, sends it from their own email, or discards it.

**Review outcome**:
What a committee member records on `/committee/review` after a Proposal Review: Approved (with the **signed Proposal**), Revisions requested, or Not approved.

**Signed Proposal**:
The approved Proposal after the reviewers sign it (in the **Sign Workshop** or a PDF editor), filed as `<Scout_Name>-<Unit>_ESSPP-ApprovedAndSignedBy-AB&CD.pdf`. With several signers each copy adds initials (`…SignedBy-AB.pdf`, then `…AB&CD.pdf`). It ends the Proposal's versions: every `ESSPP-V<n>` moves to Archive.

**New files waiting**:
A Scout sent files and no committee member has acted on them yet (hidden `_pending_since` time; amber Action cell). Cleared by moving the row, a Review outcome, or "✓ Checked: nothing to do".

**Activity**:
The tab with one row per Life to Eagle Log line, read by the **digest** (the committee's scheduled summary email).

**Staging playground**:
A copy of the tracking sheet with fake Scouts (`Scout_1`, ...) and test mode on, where committee members can try everything without affecting real Scouts or sending real email.

### Tool terms (added 2026-10-24)

**Reserved slot**:
A Review Slot the committee holds without a Scout (often for a unit hosting the board); only the committee sees it. A Scout is assigned later, which uses up the reservation.

**Unstaffed slot**:
A Review Slot with no committee member on it. Allowed (it may mark a place secured or the availability pattern to keep) but never bookable by Scouts or units until a member signs up; listed in the digest.

**Shadow booking**:
A session booked the old way (old sign-up sheet) and recorded on the new schedule so the tracking row and dashboard agree; no invitation and no email come from it. Used during the switch-over (2026-10-22 to 10-26); every type is booked online now.

**Awaiting action**:
The list at the top of every digest of things a committee member needs to do (new files, Outbox drafts, boards short of members, held submissions, unit requests, Scouts waiting to book …). Repeated until done.

**Board size**:
District/council members on an EBoR slot plus the Unit Board Members: 3 to 6. Shown as "Board n/3" (short), "n/6" (fine) or "n" (over 6).

**Unit under review**:
A unit with the Board Restriction (Unit Info → Under Review): notified of its Scouts' EBoRs but the committee fills the board; shown yellow on the dashboard.

**Unit request**:
A unit's request to hold a Scout's EBoR on its meeting or Court of Honor night, sent from the unit page; on the Admin page until Done.

**Stats History**:
The tab holding past years' numbers for the Stats page; the current year is computed live.

### Tool terms (added 2026-11-10)

**Sign Workshop**:
The Review page's in-browser signing dialog: the member signs the Proposal Approval page (typed, drawn or uploaded signature, printed name, date) and the Proposal pages' form fields are flattened; Plan, Fundraising and Report pages are never signed and stay fillable. Nothing leaves the browser until Save. The signature is an image on the page, as with the committee's earlier practice, not a certified digital signature.

**Signatures pending**:
A Proposal approved by one member while more committee signatures are to come. The copy signed so far is filed and passed to the next signer; the Scout's page says "committee signatures in progress"; the approval email is drafted after the last signer. Shown as a **Signing** tag on the dashboard.

**Preferred Name**:
The name a Scout goes by when it differs from the legal name (Theodore Sample → "Teddy"). Used in greetings and for matching; paperwork, the certificate, folders and file names keep the legal name.

**Council application**:
A Scout's Eagle Application as submitted on Council's Formsite form. The tool files the email's answers and uploads into the Scout's folder, records the match (Matched / Check / Stage? / No match) on the Council Applications tab, and moves a matched Scout from Proposal Approved to Application Submitted.

**Open-times email**:
One email To all the Scouts ready to book a session type, Cc their parents and the committee, listing the open times. Sent after the committee adds sessions.

**Lead time**:
How close to a session's start Scouts can still book it online. Each type has a default in Settings; a session may set its own (e.g. 2 hours for a weekend call a member is happy to take at short notice).

**Eagles to Date**:
A unit's count of Eagle Scouts so far (Unit Info), kept by the unit or the committee. Gives the "You are the 110th Eagle Scout of Troop 910!" line; blank means unknown, never guessed.
