Eagle Project Proposals

Submit my Eagle Project Proposal for District Review

First time sending your signed Proposal to the district. Have the Proposal from your Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook ready as a file (PDF preferred). You start with your own email address: we email you a link to the form.

Submitting starts the review; it is not an approval. The committee checks your Proposal, sets up a short Pre-Review Call, then a Proposal Review. Don't start work on the project until your Proposal is approved.
