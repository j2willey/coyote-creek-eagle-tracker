At the Board of Review, on one device: sign the Verified Eagle Application (Requirement 7). (The Advancement Report is no longer used: Council dropped it.) Each signer types their name and signs (typed or drawn). Nothing leaves this browser until Save, which files the signed PDF in the Scout's folder and drafts the email to Council in the Outbox. It also moves the Scout's row to Eagle Earned (Eagles to Date +1). Send the congratulations from the dashboard afterwards (Send congratulations… on the Eagles tab, from your phone with the photos, or later). Can't all sign here? Save partial keeps what is signed; then ask the other signers to sign in their own browser (below).
