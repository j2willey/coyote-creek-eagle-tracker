January: archive last year's Eagles

Moves the Eagle Earned rows of a past year to the top of the "Eagle Archive" tab (Settings → eagleArchiveTab) in the tracking spreadsheet, as a new "<year> Eagles" section above the older years. Columns are matched by their hidden machine name, so the archive can have its own column order; a column the archive lacks is added at its right end. Then the rows leave the tracking tab. Their folders stay where they are. Nothing happens until you confirm; the list shows what it would do.

January: archive last year's Closed records

Closed records from a past year are cleared once a year. For each: a row (no contact details) goes to the "Closed Archive" tab next to the tracking sheet, the Scout's folder moves to Archive/Closed (Settings → closedFolder), and the row leaves the tracking tab. Nothing happens until you press Archive; the list below is what it would do.

Committee members

The Committee tab: who can sign in to the committee pages, their initials on the schedule, and the Admin role (People, Settings and System tabs). Inactive = occasional helper: still put on slots and still signs in, listed under "Occasional". Nobody is deleted here: untick Active. Initials can't change once a member exists (the schedule refers to them). Every change is logged on the Activity tab.
