Sign in or start a Proposal

Enter your email address. We'll email you a link.

  • Scouts: use your own email. If we have it, the link signs you in to your page (status, updates, booking). If you're new, the link opens the form to submit your Eagle Project Proposal.

Parents and guardians: you're copied on every email to your Scout; the Scout signs in with their own address.
